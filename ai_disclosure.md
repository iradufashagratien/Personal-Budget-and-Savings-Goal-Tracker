# AI Disclosure

## Purpose

AI tools were used as a supporting resource during the development of the Personal Budget & Savings Goal Tracker.

The team remained responsible for understanding, testing, modifying, and integrating the code used in the project.

## How AI Assistance Was Used

AI assistance was used for:

* Explaining programming concepts and Python syntax.
* Discussing possible ways to structure the project into separate modules.
* Identifying missing requirements and features.
* Helping troubleshoot errors when they occurred.
* Reviewing code and suggesting improvements.
* Improving the wording and structure of project documentation.
* Helping the team understand how different modules should work together.

## Code Assistance

AI was used to discuss and improve parts of the code during development.

Any code suggestions were reviewed by the team, modified where necessary, and tested before being included in the project.

Each team member is responsible for understanding the code they contributed and being able to explain how their section works.

## Team Responsibility

AI was used as a learning and development aid rather than as a replacement for the team's work.

The team members are responsible for:

* Understanding their own code.
* Testing their features.
* Explaining their implementation.
* Integrating their modules with the rest of the application.
* Making the final decisions about what code and suggestions are used.

## AI Usage by Team Members

### Member 1 - Transaction Management

AI assistance was used for explanations, troubleshooting, and reviewing ideas related to transaction management.

### Member 2 - Savings Goals

AI assistance was used for explanations, troubleshooting, code structure discussions, and reviewing the savings goal functionality.

### Member 3 - Analysis and Reports

AI assistance was used for explanations, troubleshooting, and discussing approaches to financial calculations and reporting.

## Declaration

The team understands that AI assistance does not replace the requirement to understand the submitted work. Each member has reviewed their contribution and should be able to explain the code and decisions made in their section.
